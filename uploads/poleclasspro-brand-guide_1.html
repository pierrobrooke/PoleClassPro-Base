Section 02

ADA / WCAG 2.1 Compliance

WCAG 2.1 AA requires 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. The original hot pink on white failed at 2.3:1. Removed from all functional UI contexts.

Combination Ratio Result Use Case
#662490 on White 7.2:1 AAA Headings, nav, primary buttons
#BF1E6E on White 4.7:1 AA Links, borders, secondary CTAs
#140B26 on White 18.5:1 AAA All body text
White on Near Black 18.5:1 AAA Hero, footer, dark callouts
White on Purple 7.2:1 AAA Purple callout boxes, badges
Dark on Pink Tint 18.6:1 AAA Highlight callout boxes
White on Hot Pink 2.3:1 FAIL ❌ RETIRED — do not use

Section 04

Logo Usage

Font note The logo wordmark uses Barlow 900 — the same font as all UI headings. This creates a unified brand voice across the mark and the product. The dancer silhouette (Jasmine pose) is the proprietary element — never crop, distort, recolor, or separate her from the wordmark. Minimum logo width: 120px.
Dancer detail rule The full multi-color dancer detail (purple body, hot pink hair, blush highlights) only reads correctly on dark backgrounds (#140B26). On purple and white backgrounds, the dancer renders as a white silhouette + hot pink highlights to preserve legibility. This is intentional and systematic.

Section 06

Photography & Color Grading

Clarissa's proprietary class photos feature warm studio lighting, golden-tone hardwood floors, and bright ambient windows. Grading should enhance this warmth while pulling in the brand palette.

📸 Source Photo Profile
Warm amber floors · Natural window fill · Clean mirror walls · Soft shadow tones
🎨 Recommended Grade
Keep warm floors · Pull shadows toward #1C0E2A · Add #BF9FD4 midtone tint · Blush accent on chrome pole
⚙️ Lightroom Settings
Tone: Exposure +0.2 · Contrast +15 · Highlights -20 · Shadows +10 · Blacks -15

Color: Temp +100K · Tint +5 magenta · Purple Hue -15 · Purple Sat +20

Split Tone: Shadows Hue 280 / Sat 18 · Highlights Hue 340 / Sat 8

Sharpening: Amount 40 · Masking 60
🚫 Avoid These Grades
❌ Heavy desaturation / B&W — removes energy
❌ Teal-orange film grade — off-brand
❌ Oversaturated hot pink skin
❌ Cool/blue shadow toning
❌ Heavy vignette — dated look

Section 08

Usage Rules

  • ✅Deep Purple (#662490) is the primary heading and CTA color on all light backgrounds.
  • ✅White text on Deep Purple is approved everywhere — AAA certified.
  • ✅Rose (#BF1E6E) is the only pink approved for readable text on white surfaces.
  • ✅Hot Pink (#FF559D) may be used for decorative lines, dividers, SVG illustration — never for text over white.
  • ✅Pink Tint (#FFF0F6) callout backgrounds are approved with dark (#140B26) text.
  • ✅Logo on dark backgrounds: full multi-color dancer. Logo on purple or white: white silhouette + hot pink highlights.
  • ✅All photography should follow the warm-studio grade — preserve floor warmth, pull shadows purple.
  • ✅Minimum body font size: 14px (DM Sans). Headings: Barlow 700–900.
  • 🚫Never use Hot Pink or Blush Pink as text color on white or light backgrounds.
  • 🚫Never place the logo on a hot pink background.
  • 🚫Never distort, rotate, or recolor the dancer figure outside the approved variants.
  • 🚫Never separate the dancer silhouette from the wordmark.
  • 🚫Never use font weights below 400 for body text.

Advisory Council Note

These guidelines reflect ACE curriculum standards for professional fitness branding and the ADA compliance audit for PoleClass Pro. The hot pink callout issue is resolved at the system level — replaced with two compliant alternatives that still carry the brand's feminine energy. The dancer detail rule (dark bg = full color, colored bg = white silhouette) is documented as intentional brand behavior, not a limitation.
